# ASE Crimson Chat — Live Moving Marker v3.4

- Join the six-person game and open the public or private chat's map.
- Use **LIVE MARK / OFF** to turn on one-tap marker movement. Every map tap replaces the visible location of your dot. Only the newest **two direction arrows** remain. No note or reason is needed.
- Use **SIM INCOMING PATH**, then tap a starting point and destination. This creates a fictional red incoming-direction arrow, separate from your live dot trail. Repeat to replace the route.
- Use **CLEAR MY LIVE** to clear your own live dot and incoming route from the active chat.
- Updates share only with the selected chat. Private routes are never broadcast to public chat or another private chat.
- Live overlays disappear when their creator disconnects. Map marks, clusters, reports, photos, calls and the 20 built-in ASE MP3 sounds from v3.3 remain unchanged.

The image/map remains an illustrated roleplay reference. Neither tool uses GPS, tracks people, or controls anything in the real world.
